    P must be symmetric (P == P.T) with all eigenvalues >= 0, within numerical tolerance.

         "objective": float | None,
         "message": str}

Think step by step:
1. Identify which assets have expected returns at or above the target.
2. Note which pairs of assets have low or negative covariance (good for diversification).
3. Consider how to balance the return constraint against variance minimization.
4. Reason about whether the return constraint will bind at the optimum.
