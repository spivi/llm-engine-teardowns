You are given a Traveling Salesman Problem with time windows. There are 12 cities indexed 0..11. City 0 is the depot.

City coordinates (x, y):
{coordinates}

Travel time between cities i and j is round(euclidean_distance(i, j) / {speed}).
Service time at each non-depot city is {service_time}. The depot has no service time.

Time windows [open, close] per city:
{time_windows}

A tour starts at the depot at time = 0, visits every city exactly once, and returns to the depot.
The arrival time at the next city is (departure time from current) + (travel time). The vehicle may
wait at a city until its window opens, but cannot arrive after its window closes. Service time is
charged when departing a non-depot city.

Find a tour starting and ending at city 0 that visits every city exactly once, respects every time
window, and minimizes total elapsed time from depot departure to depot return.

Think step by step:
1. Identify cities with the tightest time windows — these constrain the tour most.
2. Group nearby cities and consider whether their windows allow a shared visit cluster.
3. Sketch a feasible sequence in your head and check whether it respects all windows before committing.
4. Look for swaps or reorderings that reduce total time while keeping all windows satisfied.

After your reasoning, return a single JSON object with one field: "tour", a list of 13 city indices starting and ending with 0.
Place the JSON on its own line at the very end of your response, preceded by the marker line `ANSWER:` on its own line.
